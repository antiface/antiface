# Alex Gagnon (A.G.) — interdisciplinary artist-researcher

I'm Alex Gagnon, an independent interdisciplinary artist-researcher based in Mont-Saint-Hilaire, Québec. For thirty years my practice has moved between painting (analog and digital), ambient and experimental sound design, and novelistic and philosophical writing — every project carried by its own conceptual scheme, all of it documented in public repositories under vocabularies I define myself.

This account — `antiface`, my handle for over a decade — is the workshop: Python tools, technical specifications, documentation systems, research apparatuses, and the occasional program that actually runs.

## What I'm working on

- **POST-ARCHIVAL** — the main project, in two branches: *The Experimental Novel* (all novelistic writing) and *The Deep-Archive Project* (software, philosophy formalizations, archival experiments).
- **The Refcards-Project** — fifteen years in the making: a simplified library card catalog for what I call *atoms of knowledge* — basically a simplified World Wide Web running locally, after Otlet's Mundaneum, HyperCard, and the online library catalog. To be clear, I haven't invented anything new; I wrote a technical specification that blends the sources into one system.
- **The Historiotheque** — my art studio, sound-design lab, and research practice treated as *cultural software*, with Official Releases under semantic versioning. Org: [Historiotheque](https://github.com/Historiotheque).
- **Atmospherics Theory** — a unified theory of atmosphere: the air around us treated as a continuous, resonant field you can listen to, record, and think with — grounded in years of daily field recordings of ambient sound.
- **Noise Field Theory / Signal Science** — a noise-based and a signal-based view of the universe and everything in it, including the "noise field" digital painting form.
- **The Switchboard Method** — how the work actually gets done: I rotate across projects in alternating 15-minute intervals, so nothing stalls and nothing depends on remembering where I left off. Everything gets logged automatically as I go.
- **Phenomenology of Art-Research Practice** (in progress) — a first-person study of what art-research feels like from the inside: the workspace, the tools within reach, the daily rhythms of making — examined the way phenomenologists examine lived experience.

## Currently building

After a few years away from serious coding, I'm back into Python — this time working *with* LLMs and agentic AI to build more complex software than I ever managed alone. The guiding idea is *Cultural Software*: programs designed as cultural objects, made for my repositories and everything around them. The daily discipline hasn't changed: hours spent reading other people's code on GitHub as deliberate practice — *reading code* is too often overlooked. On the desk: *Fluent Python*, *CPython Internals*, *Neural Networks from Scratch*.

## A note on these repositories

Most of my repositories don't contain code — they contain ideas: conceptual systems, art-research projects, novels. The documentation *is* the practice. Logging, specifying, and archiving aren't the record of the work; they're the work.

⚡ **Fun fact:** I'm an interdisciplinary artist-researcher — that's what I was in my previous life, before programming. I'm well into my second decade of code and it's funny: all that time in, I don't have much to show for it. What I have is fluency — I read and write Python the way you'd read a newspaper, internals and all. No major shipped projects; just the daily practice.
